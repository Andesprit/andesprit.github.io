YouTube Uploader

Privacy

How this personal Mac setup handles channel authorization and local data.

Operated by Andesprit · andesprit.sa@gmail.com

Purpose and permission

YouTube Uploader is a personal desktop setup for its owner’s own channels. It uses YouTube API Services and requests only youtube.upload for the channel selected during Google consent.

The connection helper uses this authorization to obtain, refresh and store the owner’s channel grant. Video uploading and automatic scheduling are not yet implemented. The setup currently has no app-operated uploading backend.

Information used and stored

The Google OAuth client configuration is stored privately on the Mac outside code repositories. Channel OAuth credentials, including refresh tokens, are stored in macOS Keychain. Local receipts and selection records identify the configured channel and authorization status without containing tokens.

The authorization flow communicates with Google to obtain and refresh the channel grant. An authorized future upload workflow would send owner-selected video files and accompanying metadata to YouTube for the chosen channel.

Google handles information received through its services under the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and manual deletion

The current installation keeps its local configuration and connection records until the owner removes them. Keychain credentials remain locally until removed or replaced. No scheduled cleanup or automatic deletion after revocation is configured.

To disconnect, revoke the app’s access through Google’s permissions settings. Revocation prevents further use of that grant; it does not erase the copy stored on the Mac.

To remove the local connection, use macOS Keychain Access to remove the stored YouTube Uploader channel credential, and delete its local connection receipts and selection record. The private client configuration can also be removed when the setup is no longer needed.

These local removals do not delete videos or information already stored by YouTube. Manage that information through YouTube.

These information pages

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in its Pages data-collection information and Privacy Statement.

Andesprit’s existing theme and language controls use browser local storage to remember those preferences. These pages use the site’s locally hosted styles, fonts and navigation script.

Contact links open your email application. These two pages do not embed the website’s contact form. Messages sent by email are handled through the sender’s email service and Andesprit’s Gmail mailbox.

Questions and changes

Contact Andesprit at andesprit.sa@gmail.com for privacy questions or deletion requests.

This disclosure must be reviewed before the setup’s permissions or data practices change.
