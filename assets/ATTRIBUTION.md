# Visual sources

## Current project illustrations

`illustrations/*.webp` contains nineteen user-commissioned AI-generated concept illustrations. Each depicts the named project's purpose, not its actual interface. Full compositions and headings are retained within the existing frames. Captions and alternative text in the bilingual catalog identify them as illustrations. They do not establish independent binary or end-to-end product testing.

`flow-atelier.webp` retains the separately approved AI-generated workflow illustration, its original 1586×992 dimensions and composition. The OpenAI, Anthropic and Grok marks were requested by the owner; their inclusion does not imply a partnership or endorsement.

Source-code licensing is separate from the illustrations. Each project's source availability and verified license evidence are recorded in `data/projects.json`.

## Existing studio assets

The existing landscape, studio mark and service illustrations retain their original roles. Archivo and Alegreya Sans are the original self-hosted font assets, unchanged. Pre-existing stock project images remain in the repository for history but are no longer used by the catalog cards.
